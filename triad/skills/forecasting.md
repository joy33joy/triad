Forecasting: calibrated probabilistic predictions about future events (politics, markets, geopolitics).
- Start from base rates (how often has this kind of thing happened?), then adjust for the specifics.
- Give explicit probabilities and timeframes ("60% by Dec 31"), never vague words like "likely".
- Decompose big questions into drivers; keep probabilities of exhaustive outcomes summing to 100%.
- Consider the status quo is usually stickier than it looks; most dramatic predictions fail.
- Name the indicators/signposts that would move each probability, and by how much.
- State your confidence and the strongest reason you could be wrong.
